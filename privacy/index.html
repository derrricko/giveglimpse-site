Privacy

What this site collects.

Almost nothing. Updated September 20, 2026.

This site

giveglimpse.com is a static site. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies set by Glimpse, and no analytics script. Nothing typed here is stored, because there is nowhere to type.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the request under Google's privacy policy.

Links to other services

Pages link to X, YouTube, pump.fun, and Paid (usepaid.app). Following a link puts the visitor under that service's terms and privacy policy, not this one. Nothing from those services runs on this site until a link is clicked.

Video clips

Short clips on the track-record page are hosted on this site and play only when pressed. No viewing data is collected.

Email

Messages sent to derrick@giveglimpse.com are kept as ordinary email and used only to reply.

Recipients and needs

When a need is listed, the recipient (an organization's name, or a person's first name only), the item, its price, and photos of the item are published on this site and on X. Photos never show a person's face. A recipient can ask for a correction at any time by email; entries are corrected, not removed.

Changes

This page changes when the site changes. The date at the top is the record.
