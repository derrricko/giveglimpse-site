Chapter one · 2021 → 2026

How I got here, in my own words.

A gas station in 2021. A video in 2024. A coin I didn't plan. An app that got told no. Every line below is dated and linked to where I said it, most of it clipped straight from the streams. Nothing hidden, nothing deleted.

A few words, if you're new: Solana is a blockchain. The Seeker is Solana's phone. pump.fun is a site where anyone can launch a coin and stream live. A dApp Store is an app store for blockchain apps.
