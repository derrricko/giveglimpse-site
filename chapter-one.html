Chapter one · 2021 → 2026

How Glimpse got here, in the founder's own words.

A gas station in 2021. A video in 2024. A coin he didn't plan. An app that got told no. Every line below is dated and sourced, most of it from the streams themselves. Nothing hidden, nothing deleted.

A few words, for anyone new: Solana is a blockchain. The Seeker is Solana's phone. pump.fun is a site where anyone can launch a coin and stream live. A dApp Store is an app store for blockchain apps.

PrologueBefore the app2021 → 2025
Late November 2021 · a gas station in Iowa

Emmanuel.

"I ran into an Ethiopian migrant at the gas station. It was late November and it was really cold. I was on break from college, and I was prompted by the Lord to give him a ride."

"I gave him a car. It was a $3,500 Toyota that I put new tires on and paid for the first few months of insurance, but this changed the trajectory of his life."

Derrick, on stream, Mar 2 and Feb 25, 2026

His life didn't change because of a gala or a matching program. "It's because one singular person showed up. That's the goal of Glimpse."

January 24, 2024

The original video.

"I walked around with this piece of paper in the front of my Carhartt for probably six months to a year, hoping that someone would randomly stop me on the street and I would be able to explain to them the seven-page document of how I plan to change the world. And that's the first time I've ever said that out loud."

"This will reshape the crypto industry," 17:16
28 minutes, two years before the first line of code.Jan 2024

He'd tried it as an NFT project in 2022. "None of it worked because the tech wasn't ready, the tooling wasn't ready, and I couldn't find a technical founder. And I tried to fundraise and that didn't work."

2023 → 2025

Three years door to door.

"I door-knocked for three years. Coding for fourteen to sixteen hours a day and building this app is a walk in the park."

On stream, Feb 27, 2026

Married. A son, October 2024. A sales job. And the same seven pages.

Act IIIThe costMar → Aug 2026

Not for the faint of heart.

"Submitting an app is not for the faint of heart. V1 of the app is supposed to be bare bones. From here we build from the ground up. There are many unknowns. Only the beginning."

Spring 2026

What it cost.

"It was very hard accepting the fact that my idea just was not good enough. I wanted to will it to success, but at times it felt like pushing a square peg into a round hole."

Derrick, September 2026

He made nothing on the launch and lost what he put in. Months of not sleeping over people with no faces. Lesson kept: speculators are not donors. Belief follows proof.

July 6, 2026

Grateful, never greedy.

"I have a kid, a wife, and I start a new role in AI training and enablement at a Fortune 1000. The goal is the same. I am going to make it. Same grinder from 2021 with a different mentality. Grateful, never greedy."

@DerrickWKing, Jul 6

Over the summer, a rebuilt engine: verified receipts, proof photos, one need at a time. Nonprofits found. None funded. Moving the money was the wall.
