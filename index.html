01 / THE MISSIONGLIMPSE / OPEN RECORD

We believe giving should be simple, effective, and transparent.

Our ambition is to find the most effective use of a dollar in the world. We start with practical needs, work with the people who can help, and learn from what actually happens.

THE STANDARD WE HOLD OURSELVES TO

Three questions guide the work.

GLIMPSE / THE OPEN RECORD[g]
01A CLEAR NEED
02USEFUL HELP
03A VISIBLE RESULT

What does
someone need?

A specific need and a clear way to help. Anyone should be able to understand the undertaking before deciding to take part.

What will
actually help?

Choose practical work that meets the need. Look at the full cost and what it accomplished, then use what we learn to improve.

Can we see
what happened?

Keep the commitment, spending, delivery, and unfinished work in an open record. Show the follow-through while protecting the people receiving help.

DECISIONS / IN THE OPEN
What role will holders have?

We intend for holder input to help shape which needs Glimpse takes on. Eligibility, voting methods, and how input is weighed remain open as we seek regulatory clarity and legal guidance. No binding voting or ownership rights are promised.

Who makes the final decision?

Derrick retains final responsibility for company spending and execution. The aim is to publish community input, the chosen direction, and the reasons behind it.

Will Glimpse become a nonprofit?

Creating a nonprofit or partnering with an existing one are options being explored. Neither route is confirmed. Glimpse is currently a media business.

What will “effective” mean?

We will need to show the full cost, what was delivered, whether it met the intended need, and what we learned. “Most effective” is the ambition, not a verified ranking.
