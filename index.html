How it works

01

Someone names one need under $500 and sends one photo.

A person, a family, an organization. No wallet, no form, no bank account required. The item shows up.

02

Trading fees become dollars.

Paid claims the creator fees on chain and pays 80% to the founder's X Money account at each milestone, from $5 up to $1,000.

The other 20% is Paid's, used to buy and burn its own token. Paid's rule for every token on its rail.

03

The need is bought and the proof is posted.

Posted, funded, bought, proven. Same four words every time. A late photo shows as "proof overdue" until it arrives. Nothing is deleted.

Where the money is, at each step
On chain
A trade on $GIVE leaves a creator fee. It sits in the token's fee account until claimed.
Paid
Paid claims it, converts it to dollars, and holds the dollars in its own X Money balance until a payout milestone.Paid's custody, Paid's rules. Its capital-flow page.
The founder
The payout lands in Derrick Woepking's personal X Money account, and Paid replies publicly on X with the amount.Personal income. Not a donation, not tax-deductible for anyone.
The store
The exact item that was named gets bought. The dated receipt gets posted.
The recipient
Receives the item and sends a photo of it in use. The entry is marked proven.

Fees first. Donations later.

No donations are taken today. That is the design.

Fees fund the needs while the loop is proven in public. Donations open after that, under a structure built to hold other people's money. The plan, the evidence, and the commitments are on the vision page.
