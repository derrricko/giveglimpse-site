How it works
01

Nonprofits do one thing.

Name a need and send a photo. No wallet, no form, no bank rail, no account. The item shows up.

02

Fees do the funding.

Every trade on $GIVE leaves a small fee behind. Paid routes it to the founder's X Money. Nobody has to decide to give.Paid pays out as the balance crosses $5, $10, $20, $50, $100, $250, $500, $1,000.

03

X is the ledger.

Each payout is confirmed in public by @UsePaid. Each purchase and each photo lands in the need's thread. Nothing hidden, nothing deleted.
