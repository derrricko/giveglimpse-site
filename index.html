How it works

01

Someone names a need and sends a photo.

A person, a family, an organization. No wallet, no form, no account.

02

Fees from $GIVE pay for it.

Every trade leaves a small fee. Paid turns the fees into dollars and posts each payout in public.

03

It gets bought, and the proof gets posted.

The receipt, then the photo of it in use. Posted, funded, bought, proven. A late photo says "proof overdue" until it arrives.
