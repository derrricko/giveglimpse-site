How it works

Nonprofits do one thing

Name a need and send a photo. No wallet, no form, no bank rail, no account. The item shows up.

Fees do the funding

Every trade on $GIVE leaves a small fee behind. Paid routes it to the founder's X Money. Nobody has to decide to give.

X is the ledger

Each payout is confirmed in public by @UsePaid. Each purchase and each photo lands in the need's thread.

The honest part

Fees first. Donations later.

Right now there is no donor. Fees from $GIVE come to Derrick Woepking personally through X Money, and he buys the need himself. Every dollar in and out is posted.

Donations open once this is proven safe and real, under a structure built for it. Nothing is promised to anyone who holds $GIVE.
