Why a second token?

We launched $GIVE because we could not direct the creator fees from our first token, $GLIMPSE, to Paid. The two tokens are separate; $GLIMPSE is no longer part of Glimpse.

How $GIVE fees work

A creator fee is a small amount set aside by the trading platform. Paid (@UsePaid) converts $GIVE creator fees into dollars and pays Derrick through X Money:

1 · Trade
Someone buys or sells $GIVE on pump.fun. The platform sets aside a creator fee.
2 · Convert
Paid collects the fee, converts it to dollars, and posts each payout in public on its payments page.
3 · Receive
The dollars land in Derrick's X Money account as his income.
4 · Spend
Derrick buys what the undertaking needs and posts the receipt.

The exact routing and any service deductions along the way will be published here once verified.

These fees are Derrick's personal income, not company revenue. $GIVE is not an investment and confers no rights.

Local business advertising

Glimpse plans to earn revenue from businesses advertising to the audience of its show and stories. Paid placements will be identified. No advertisers or revenue totals are claimed here.

Advertising revenue supports the company. No fixed future allocation or reinvestment percentage is promised.
