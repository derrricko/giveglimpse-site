THE DETAIL
MATTERS.

For each event: the commitments, the money received, what was delivered, production cost, fees, and anything unfinished. Company revenue and designated contributions must remain distinguishable.

Footage provides context. Receipts and payment records explain spending. Neither should expose private recipient information.

Every undertaking moves through four words: posted → funded → bought → proven. The same four words head each thread on @GiveGlimpse.

PROVEN

Completed.

Bought, delivered, and the supporting evidence is linked.

PENDING

Still to do.

A recorded commitment has not been completed.

PROOF OVERDUE

Evidence is late.

An item is recorded as delivered, but expected supporting evidence has not arrived.

Status definitions only. These are not actual event entries.

What will a record entry contain?

Item or service; intended purpose without identifying a recipient; funding source; amount; fees; status; date; and evidence link. Corrections should retain the original record and explain the change.

What if the total is zero?

A confirmed zero should appear as $0. Missing or unpublished data should say so. They are different states.
