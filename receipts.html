The ledger
#NeedOrgAmountStatusDateLinks

Queued means the item is named and priced but the org hasn't confirmed yet. Orgs are named when they confirm. A need enters the ledger proper when it is posted on @GiveGlimpse with a photo.

How to read a row
Posted
The org, the item, the price, a photo of the need. Never a face.
Funded
The fee payout that covers it, linked to Paid's public reply on X.
Bought
The store receipt, dated, card digits covered.
Proven
The org's photo of the item in use.
Overdue
Bought, and the photo hasn't come. Stays marked until it does.
Where the money is, at each step
On chain
A trade on $GIVE leaves a creator fee in the token's fee account.
Paid
Claims the fee, converts to dollars, holds until a payout milestone. Their public payments ledger.
Me
Payout lands in my personal X Money account. Paid replies on X with the amount. That reply is the "funded" link in each row.
The store
I buy the exact item. The dated receipt is the "bought" link.
The nonprofit
Sends the photo. That post is the "proven" link.
