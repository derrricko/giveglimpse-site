About

The trust layer for giving.

Glimpse funds real needs and proves it. Someone names a need and sends a photo. It gets bought. The receipt and the photo of it in use are posted where anyone can see them, and nothing is ever deleted.

Who builds it

Glimpse is built by Derrick Woepking in Muscatine, Iowa. He has worked on it since 2021 and built the current version himself. Fees from $GIVE are paid to him directly and in public, and he buys each need himself.

@DerrickWKing · @GiveGlimpse · derrick@giveglimpse.com

Where it started

A cold night in November 2021, a man outside a gas station in Iowa who needed a ride, and then a used car so he could get to work. His life changed because one person showed up. Glimpse exists to make that ordinary, and visible.

The vision, on record in January 2024, two years before the first line of code: watch.

What Glimpse commits to

01

Named items only.

Every need is a specific thing, priced, for a specific recipient. No cash drops.

02

A photo for every need.

Of the need before, and of the item in use after. Never a face.

03

Every payout is public.

Paid replies on X with each payout. Each purchase is a dated store receipt. Each entry links to its thread.

04

Nothing is deleted.

A late photo shows as "proof overdue." An entry that fails stays on the list with its status.

05

Donations open only when the structure is ready.

Fees fund the needs until the loop has run in public long enough to trust. Then donations, under an entity built to hold other people's money.

Where this goes

Any person, any country, any cause.

The operating system for global generosity. See what your money did, every time.
