The vision

A better way to give. Built for the people on both ends of it.

Giving spreads when people can see it. Proof is what makes it last. So the unit of Glimpse is the receipt, not the donation: one real need, met, and shown.

A better way

A need is named before a dollar moves. The dollar comes from trading fees, so nobody has to be asked. The item is bought, not wired. The photo closes it. Then the next need goes up.

01
A trade on $GIVE leaves a fee. Small, automatic, every time.
02
The fee buys a need. One recipient, one item, named in advance.
03
The receipt is posted. Posted, funded, bought, proven, with the photo.
04
The receipt is the content. A real thing, bought for a real organization, shown. That is what people share.
05
The content brings the next trade. Which leaves the next fee. Which buys the next need.

Content drives fees. Proof drives more. In chapter one the distribution was a phone: 116,000 Seekers in 50 countries. In chapter two the distribution is X, the largest social app in the world, and Paid is the rail that turns attention into dollars.

A campaign ends. The Ice Bucket Challenge raised $220 million in 2014 and about $500,000 when it was rerun in 2015. A loop doesn't end. Every need is its own proof event, and the next one starts the day the photo lands.

Commitments

01

Named items only.

Every need is a specific thing, priced, for a specific organization. No cash drops.

02

A photo for every need.

Of the need before, and of the item in use after. Never a face.

03

Every payout is public.

Paid replies on X with each payout. Each purchase is a dated store receipt. Each entry links to its thread.

04

Nothing is deleted.

A late photo shows as "proof overdue." An entry that fails stays on the list with its status. The list on this site and the posts on X are the record.

05

Donations open only when the structure is ready.

Fees fund the needs until the loop has run in public long enough to trust. Then donations, under an entity built to hold other people's money.

In order

Now

Fees fund needs while the loop is proven in public.

No donor, so nobody's money is at risk while the loop is proven. The fees are the founder's income, and he buys each need himself.

Next

Donations.

They open once the loop has run safe and real for long enough to trust, under a structure built to hold other people's money.

Then

Any person, any country, any cause.

The operating system for global generosity. See what your money did, every time.
